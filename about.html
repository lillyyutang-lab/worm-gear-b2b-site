WHO WE ARE

Built for reliable industrial motion

Founded in 2010, SMK Transmission manufactures industrial power transmission products for distributors, original equipment manufacturers and engineering buyers. We support both new machinery projects and replacement applications.

Our range includes NMRV, NRV, PCRV and DRV worm gear reducers, BKM hypoid gearboxes, AC and DC gear motors, three-phase motors, single-phase motors, brake motors and matched motor-gearbox assemblies.

From product selection to drawings, configuration and export delivery, our goal is to make industrial sourcing clear, dependable and efficient.
