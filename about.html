About SMK Transmission

Power transmission manufacturing and application support since 2010

A Professional Gearbox and Motor Manufacturer

SMK Transmission was founded in 2010 as a manufacturer of industrial power transmission products. We supply distributors, original equipment manufacturers and engineering buyers with gear reducers, gear motors and electric motors for new equipment and replacement applications.

Our product portfolio includes NMRV, NRV, PCRV and DRV worm gear reducers; BKM hypoid gearboxes; AC and DC gear motors; three-phase motors; single-phase motors; brake motors; and matched motor-gearbox assemblies.

What We Manufacture

  • Worm gear reducers and NMRV gearboxes
  • BKM hypoid gearboxes
  • AC and DC gear motors
  • Three-phase and single-phase electric motors
  • Brake motors and customized drive assemblies

Applications We Support

  • Conveyors and material handling
  • Packaging and food-processing machinery
  • Agricultural and lifting equipment
  • Automation and production lines
  • Mixers, pumps and general industrial machinery

Engineering Support

SMK helps customers evaluate output speed, ratio, torque, service factor, mounting position, input power and operating environment. OEM and ODM support is available for compatible configurations, branding and packaging.

Global Market

We serve international B2B customers and have export experience across Europe, South America, Asia and the Middle East. Buyers can request drawings, catalog data and selection assistance before ordering.
