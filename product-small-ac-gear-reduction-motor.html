Components

1. Housing:
Frame size 18–28: aluminium alloy ADC12.
Frame size 32–50: cast iron HT200.

2. Gears: Helical gears, 2 or 3 stage.

3. Input Configurations:
GH/GV Series – compact motor, single phase / three phase, optional brake.
GHM/GVM Series – IEC flange.
GHD/GVD Series – solid shaft input.

4. Output Configurations:
Solid shaft output.

Features

  1. Compact structure.
  2. Wide range of gear ratios; ratios 250–1800 are available through two-frame-size combinations.
  3. Different input options available, with flexible mounting arrangements and positions.
  4. Three-phase or single-phase motors are available with optional brake, aluminium alloy housing, TEFC IP54/IP55 aluminium alloy terminal box, dustproof and waterproof construction.
