NMRV130 Worm Gearbox

Installation dimensions, ratios, motor flange options and selection guide

Published October 9, 2026 · SMK Transmission buyer FAQ

Buyer answer: A reliable OEM worm gearbox manufacturer should be able to confirm the selected model with drawings and application data—not only quote a product name. Review the supplier’s product range, technical communication, factory evidence, quality process and support for the configuration you actually need.

Q1: How can I find a reliable worm gearbox manufacturer in China?

Start by reviewing product coverage, manufacturing and assembly capability, technical documentation and customer support. A supplier should be able to provide product specifications, dimension drawings and a quotation that clearly identifies the proposed model and configuration.

For an NMRV/RV reducer, compare the size, ratio, output bore, motor interface, mounting position and required duty—not only the product photo. The NMRV worm gearbox product page and the RV25–RV150 size guide can help structure this comparison.

Q2: How can I verify if a Chinese gearbox supplier is a real factory?

Ask for current factory or assembly photos, production videos, business information and manufacturing details. A live video visit can help you see the production and assembly process. For a larger project, an independent factory inspection can provide an additional check.

Practical check: Factory evidence does not replace technical validation. Confirm the exact ratio, output arrangement, motor flange and drawing for the model you plan to purchase.

Q3: Can Chinese manufacturers provide customized NMRV worm gearboxes?

Yes, OEM configurations may include output shaft or bore dimensions, input flange dimensions, gear ratio, housing color, motor-and-gearbox matching and private-label branding. Availability depends on the selected gearbox model and technical requirements.

For a direct replacement, provide the existing nameplate and dimensions. See the worm gearbox replacement checklist for the key details to collect before requesting a quotation.

Q4: What should I check before purchasing an NMRV worm gearbox?

  • Gearbox size and reduction ratio
  • Required output torque and duty cycle
  • Input motor power and rated speed
  • Output shaft or bore dimensions, keyway and tolerance
  • Motor flange, pilot and bolt pattern
  • Mounting position and operating conditions

Correct selection helps reduce the risk of overheating, premature wear and installation mismatch. Use the ratio and torque calculator for a preliminary speed estimate, then confirm the service factor and actual load with the supplier.

Q5: What is the minimum order quantity for OEM worm gearboxes?

Minimum order quantity depends on the model, customization scope and packaging requirements. Standard models and customized units should be quoted separately. Ask whether a sample order can be supported for initial evaluation; do not assume an MOQ without a written quotation.

Q6: Where can I find an OEM NMRV worm gearbox supplier in China?
