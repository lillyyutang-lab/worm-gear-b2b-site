NMRV040 Dimensions & RV40 Selection Guide

18 mm output bore, ratios 5–100 and motor flange dimensions

Published September 23, 2026 · Reviewed by SMK Transmission

Quick answer: The NMRV040 (RV40) is a compact aluminum worm gearbox with a standard 18 mm hollow output bore. Standard ratios range from 5:1 to 100:1, with 11 mm and 14 mm input-bore options for different motor sizes. Square flange options may also be used for compatible stepper and servo motors.

NMRV040 Installation Dimensions

The NMRV040 is a compact right-angle worm gearbox suitable for conveyors, packaging equipment, automation systems and other small industrial machines.

What Ratios Are Available for RV40?

Standard illustrated ratios are 5, 7.5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80 and 100:1.

Theoretical output speed = motor speed ÷ reduction ratio

Example: 1400 rpm motor and 50:1 ratio: 1400 ÷ 50 = approximately 28 rpm.

Actual output speed may vary with motor rated speed, load and operating conditions.

How to Select the Right RV40

Confirm motor power, input speed, required output speed, shaft diameter and mounting position. For replacement applications, send the existing gearbox nameplate and installation dimensions. Review the NMRV size guide, mounting-position guide, ratio and torque calculator and NMRV worm gearbox range for related checks.
