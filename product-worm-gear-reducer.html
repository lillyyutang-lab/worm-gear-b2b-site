RV Series Worm Gearboxes

SMK RV series worm gearboxes combine a compact right-angle housing with flexible motor and output configurations. They are selected by required ratio, output torque, mounting position, shaft dimensions, duty cycle and motor interface.

Components

1. Housing: Die-cast aluminum alloy gearbox for RV025–RV090; cast iron gearbox for RV110–RV150.

2. Worm wheel: Wear-resistant tin (stannum) bronze alloy or aluminum bronze alloy, subject to model.

3. Worm shaft: 20Cr steel with carburizing, quenching and grinding; surface hardness 56–62 HRC, with approximately 0.3–0.5 mm remaining carburized layer after precision grinding.

4. Input configurations: Electric motor, brake motor, DC motor or servo motor; IEC B5/B14 motor flange, NEMA C-face motor flange and solid shaft input options are available subject to model.

5. Output configurations: Keyed hollow shaft output, hollow shaft with output flange or plug-in solid shaft output.

6. Options: Worm shaft rear extension, single output shaft, double output shaft, output flange, torque arm and dust cover.

7. Gearbox painting: Aluminum alloy housings receive shot blasting, anticorrosion treatment and phosphating, then RAL 5010 gentian blue or RAL 7035 light grey paint. Cast iron housings receive red antirust treatment and RAL 5010 gentian blue paint.

Models

Hollow shaft input with IEC-normalized motor flange: RV025–RV150.

Solid shaft input: RV025–RV150.

Features

  • Quality aluminum alloy gearbox options with low weight and corrosion-resistant surface treatment.
  • Two applicable worm-wheel material options: tin bronze or aluminum bronze alloy.
  • Standard parts with flexible shaft configurations and motor-flange interfaces.
  • Several mounting options for different machine layouts.
  • Low noise and efficient heat dissipation when correctly selected and lubricated.
