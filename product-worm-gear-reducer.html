RV Series Selection Summary

RV worm gear reducers provide compact 90-degree speed reduction for conveyors, packaging machines, food-processing equipment, agricultural machinery, gates, mixers, lifting mechanisms and automation systems. Select the frame size and ratio from the required output speed, torque, duty cycle, mounting position and motor interface.

Smaller RV sizes are commonly used for compact drives, while larger sizes provide higher torque capacity and larger shaft dimensions. Final selection must confirm permissible torque, service factor, thermal capacity, shaft or flange dimensions and the actual operating conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a worm gearbox?

A worm gearbox is a compact right-angle reducer using a worm shaft and worm wheel to reduce speed and increase torque.

How do I select a worm gearbox?

Check ratio, output torque, service factor, duty, mounting position, input power and operating environment.

Worm gearbox or hypoid gearbox?

Choose worm gearing for compact high-ratio reduction; consider hypoid gearing when higher efficiency and continuous duty are priorities.

Are worm gearboxes self-locking?

Not always. Never rely on presumed self-locking for personnel or load safety; specify a suitable brake or holding device.
