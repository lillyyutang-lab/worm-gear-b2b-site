Product Introduction

SMK Transmission manufactures NMRV, NRV, PCRV and DRV worm gear reducers. A worm shaft drives a worm wheel at 90 degrees, reducing output speed and increasing available torque. The compact layout is widely used where installation space, quiet operation and a broad choice of ratios are important.

Advantages

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a worm gearbox?

A worm gearbox is a compact right-angle reducer using a worm shaft and worm wheel to reduce speed and increase torque.

How do I select a worm gearbox?

Check ratio, output torque, service factor, duty, mounting position, input power and operating environment.

Worm gearbox or hypoid gearbox?

Choose worm gearing for compact high-ratio reduction; consider hypoid gearing when higher efficiency and continuous duty are priorities.

Are worm gearboxes self-locking?

Not always. Never rely on presumed self-locking for personnel or load safety; specify a suitable brake or holding device.
