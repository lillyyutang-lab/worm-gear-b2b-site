The RV50 worm gearbox is used in compact industrial drive systems where the motor must turn a machine at a lower speed through a right-angle output. It can be supplied as a reducer or matched with an electric motor as a complete geared motor assembly.

What is an RV50 worm gearbox?

RV50 refers to a size 50 worm gear reducer. Depending on the product naming system, buyers may also see NMRV050. The unit uses a worm shaft and worm wheel to reduce speed and increase usable output torque while changing the drive direction by 90 degrees.

The size designation identifies the product family, not a complete selection. Two RV50 units with different ratios or input flanges can have different operating characteristics. Always confirm the full model code and dimensional drawing.

How to select an RV50 gearbox

  1. Calculate the required ratio. Divide motor rated speed by required machine output speed.
  2. Determine design torque. Multiply required machine torque by the application service factor.
  3. Verify the RV50 rating. Compare design torque with the manufacturer's rating at the selected ratio and input speed.
  4. Check thermal duty. Consider operating hours, ambient temperature, ventilation, starts and nearby heat sources.
  5. Confirm interfaces. Verify IEC motor frame, flange, pilot, input shaft, output bore or shaft, keyway and mounting orientation.
  6. Review safety. Do not rely on assumed self-locking for lifting or personnel-safety applications; use an engineered brake or holding device where required.

Frequently asked questions

What ratios are commonly available for RV50?

Common nominal ratios include 7.5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80 and 100. Confirm the selected configuration before ordering.

What is the output speed at 30:1?

With a 1400 rpm rated motor, theoretical output speed is approximately 46.7 rpm.

Can RV50 be supplied with a motor?

Yes. Confirm motor power, speed, IEC frame, flange, voltage, frequency and duty together with the reducer.

Is RV50 suitable for continuous duty?

It may be when mechanical and thermal ratings meet the application. Verify load, ratio, ambient conditions, lubrication and operating hours.
