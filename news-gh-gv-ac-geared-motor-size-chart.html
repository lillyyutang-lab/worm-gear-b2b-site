AC GEARED MOTOR DATA

GH/GV AC Geared Motor Size Chart

Model sizes, output shafts, power, ratios and combination limits

Published September 22, 2026 · SMK Transmission engineering data page

Buyer answer: SMK GH/GV coaxial AC geared motors cover GH18/GV18 through GH50/GV50, 0.1–3.7 kW, and output shaft diameters from 18 to 50 mm. Standard ratios are 3–200; ratios 250–1800 require combined stages. Not every motor power, frame and ratio can be combined, so torque, thermal capacity, service factor, voltage, frequency, mounting and brake requirements must be confirmed for the application.

Power and Ratio Range

  • Documented power range: 0.1–3.7 kW.
  • Standard ratio range: 3–200.
  • Combined ratio range: 250–1800 through combined stages/configurations.
Configuration limit: These ranges do not mean every power, model and ratio can be ordered in any combination. Final selection must verify rated output torque, thermal capacity, service factor, voltage, frequency, mounting, brake duty and external shaft loads.

Calculate the Preliminary Ratio

Theoretical output speed = motor rated speed ÷ total ratio

Example assumptions: motor rated speed 1,400 rpm; gearbox ratio 50:1; no allowance for motor slip or load variation.

1,400 ÷ 50 = 28 rpm theoretical output speed.

The actual output speed may differ because induction-motor speed changes with supply frequency, pole count and load. For a broader selection workflow, use the AC geared motor selection guide.

Preliminary Output Torque Formula

Tout ≈ 9550 × P × i × η ÷ nin

P = motor power in kW; i = total ratio; η = verified total drive efficiency; nin = input speed in rpm.

Do not insert an assumed efficiency into a purchasing specification. Use the formula only for preliminary comparison, then verify the available model's rated output torque and thermal capacity. Duty severity is covered in the service-factor guide.

When to Choose GH or GV

  • GH: foot mounting on a rigid machine base.
  • GV: flange mounting directly to the driven machine.

For conveyor projects, confirm drum or sprocket speed, starting load, hours per day and external shaft load. See the conveyor gearbox application page. Motor voltage, frequency and enclosure details should also be checked against the SMK electric motor range.

Selection Data to Send

  • Application and load type
  • Required output speed and continuous/peak torque
  • Motor power, voltage, frequency and phase
  • Operating hours and starts per hour
  • Foot or flange mounting and shaft direction
  • Output shaft diameter and external radial/axial load
  • Brake, VFD and ambient requirements

Conclusion

The GH/GV size chart narrows the mechanical envelope, but it does not replace application verification. Send the operating data above so the power, ratio, model, thermal capacity and mounting can be checked as one system.
