WORM GEARBOX INSTALLATION

Why Should You Replace the Breather Plug Before Using a Worm Gearbox?

A small startup step that helps equalize pressure and protect gearbox seals

Published September 7, 2026 · Reviewed by SMK Transmission

Buyer answer: If your worm gearbox is designed for vented operation, replace the sealed shipping plug with the supplied breather plug before startup. The breather allows internal pressure to equalize as the gearbox heats up, helping reduce the risk of oil leakage and excessive pressure on the oil seals.

Before starting a worm gearbox, there is one small but important step that is often overlooked: replacing the sealed shipping plug with the breather plug when the reducer is designed for vented operation.

What Does a Gearbox Breather Plug Do?

During operation, friction between the worm, worm wheel, bearings and lubricant generates heat. As the temperature inside the gearbox increases, the air and lubricant expand. The gearbox breather plug provides a controlled way for internal pressure to equalize with the outside atmosphere.

Without proper ventilation, pressure may build up inside the gearbox housing.

What Happens If You Do Not Replace the Shipping Plug?

If a gearbox designed for ventilation continues operating with a sealed shipping plug, increased internal pressure may contribute to:

  • Oil leakage around the input or output shaft seals
  • Increased pressure on oil seals and gaskets
  • Lubricant leakage from sealing points
  • Premature seal wear
  • Reduced gearbox service life

If a newly installed worm gear reducer starts leaking oil, the breather plug is therefore one of the first installation items worth checking.

Why Use a Sealed Plug During Shipping?

A gearbox may be tilted, rotated or placed in different positions during transportation. If the breather plug is installed before shipping, lubricant may escape through the vent. A sealed shipping plug helps keep the lubricant inside the gearbox during transportation and storage.

Once the gearbox reaches the customer and is installed in its final operating position, the correct breather plug can be fitted according to the manufacturer's instructions.

How to Install the Breather Plug Correctly

Before operating an NMRV worm gearbox, first confirm the mounting position. The breather should normally be installed at the designated upper position of the gearbox. The positions of the breather plug, oil-level plug and drain plug may change depending on the mounting orientation.

Mounting position→Breather plug→Oil level→Rotation direction→Test run

Important: Never assume that any threaded hole can be used for the breather. Follow the installation instructions and lubrication diagram for the specific gearbox model and mounting position.

Does Every Worm Gearbox Need a Breather Plug?

No. Some small gearboxes are designed as sealed units and may not require an external breather. However, if your gearbox is supplied with a sealed shipping plug and a separate breather plug, make sure the correct plug is installed before operation according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Conclusion

Replacing a shipping plug with a breather plug takes only a few minutes, but it is an important part of correct worm gearbox installation. Proper ventilation helps equalize internal pressure, protect oil seals, reduce the risk of lubricant leakage and support reliable gearbox operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should the shipping plug be replaced before startup?

On a gearbox designed for vented operation, the supplied breather allows expanding air to escape and helps prevent excessive pressure on seals.

Can a sealed shipping plug cause oil leakage?

Excess internal pressure can contribute to leakage at seals or other sealing points. Also check oil level, mounting position, seal condition and operating temperature.

Where is the breather installed?

Normally at the designated upper plug position for the selected mounting orientation. Refer to the model-specific instructions.

Does every gearbox require a breather?

No. Some small reducers are designed as sealed units. Follow the instructions supplied with the exact gearbox.
