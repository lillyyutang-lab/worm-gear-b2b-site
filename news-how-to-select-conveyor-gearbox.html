How To Select The Right Gearbox For Conveyor Systems

Stability, Efficiency, and Longevity Guide

August 7, 2026

Choosing the right gearbox for a conveyor system is essential for ensuring stable operation, energy efficiency, and long service life. A properly selected conveyor gearbox can reduce maintenance costs, improve production efficiency, and prevent unexpected downtime.

As a professional manufacturer specializing in worm gearboxes, hypoid gearboxes, gear motors, and electric motors since 2010, SMK Transmission provides reliable power transmission solutions for conveyor systems in various industries.

In this guide, we will explain the key factors to consider when selecting a gearbox for conveyor applications.

1. Understand The Conveyor Application Requirements

The main factors include load weight, operating speed, running time, installation space, and the working environment. For example, food processing conveyors may require compact and low-noise gearboxes, while heavy-duty industrial conveyors require high torque and strong load capacity.

2. Calculate Required Torque

Torque is critical. The gearbox must provide enough output torque to move the load safely. The basic relationship is: Torque = Force × Radius. Engineers also consider belt tension, material weight, and a safety factor.

3. Select The Correct Gearbox Ratio

The ratio determines output speed. Example: A motor at 1400 RPM with a 50:1 ratio provides ~28 RPM. Common ratios include 10:1, 20:1, 30:1, 50:1, and 100:1.

4. Choose The Right Gearbox Type

  • Worm Gearbox: Compact, low noise, cost-effective. Typical models: NMRV30 to NMRV090. Ideal for light/medium duty.
  • Hypoid Gearbox: Higher efficiency, lower energy consumption, stronger torque output. Suitable for continuous or heavy-duty operation.
