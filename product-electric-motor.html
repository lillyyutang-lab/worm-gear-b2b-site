Frequently Asked Questions

Three-phase or single-phase motor?

Three-phase motors are generally preferred for industrial sites with three-phase power. Single-phase motors suit installations where only single-phase supply is available.

Can SMK match a motor to a gearbox?

Yes. Provide input supply, output speed, torque, duty and mounting needs so the motor and reducer can be checked as a system.

When is a brake motor required?

A brake motor is commonly specified when rapid stopping, position holding or load control is needed. Safety requirements must be assessed for the machine.
