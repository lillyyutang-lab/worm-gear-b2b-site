220/380 V Delta/Star Connection

For a motor specifically marked 220/380 V Δ/Y, the normal running connection is delta at 220 V and star at 380 V. Each winding then receives approximately its intended voltage. Delta on a 380 V supply can over-voltage the windings; star on a 220 V supply can produce weak torque and overheating under load.

Always follow the connection diagram on the actual nameplate or terminal-cover label. A voltage pair alone should not be used to guess the terminal links. See the motor overheating troubleshooting guide for a systematic diagnostic sequence.

Ask an engineer: Send the motor nameplate, supply voltage and frequency, required power or load data, mounting form and application. For a replacement, include clear photos of the shaft, flange and installation.

Industrial Applications

Three phase motors are commonly used in conveyors, pumps, fans, packaging machinery, agricultural equipment, woodworking machinery, automation systems and complete gear-motor assemblies. Selection must reflect the actual load profile: a fan, conveyor and high-inertia machine can require different starting and duty checks even when their nominal kW values are similar.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I select a three phase electric motor?

Confirm shaft power and torque, supply voltage and frequency, speed or pole count, duty, starting method, mounting, enclosure and ambient conditions. For gearbox applications, also provide required output speed and torque.

What information should I send for a motor quotation?

Send required power, voltage, frequency, speed or pole count, mounting form, shaft or flange interface, duty, application and quantity. For replacement projects, include the old nameplate and installation photos.

What does 220/380 V delta/star mean?

For a motor specifically marked 220/380 V Δ/Y, the normal running connection is delta at 220 V and star at 380 V. Always follow the diagram on the actual motor nameplate.

Can SMK match a motor to a gearbox?

Yes. Provide supply, required output speed and torque, duty, mounting and environment so the motor and reducer can be checked as one system.

Can I request a brake motor or single-phase motor?

Single-phase and brake motor configurations are available for applicable models. Confirm the application, supply and stopping or holding requirement before selection.

Is the largest motor power always the safest choice?

No. Oversizing can increase purchase cost and reduce operating efficiency. Select from measured load, starting torque, duty and service conditions.
