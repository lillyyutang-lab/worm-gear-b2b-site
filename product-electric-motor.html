SMK YE3 series three phase AC asynchronous induction motors adopt IEC standards and IE3 premium efficiency. The series features a square aluminum housing, detachable mounting feet, dustproof and waterproof construction, low noise, low vibration, energy saving and long service life.

Components

1. Housing: Die-casting aluminium.

2. Shaft: 40Cr steel.

3. Magnet Wires: QZ-2/155, QZ-2/180.

4. Stator & Rotor Core: Silicon steel sheets DW470, DW600.

5. Bearings: C&U, NSK, SKF.
