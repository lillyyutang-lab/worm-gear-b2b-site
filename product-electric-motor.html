Industrial Electric Motors

Three-phase, single-phase and brake motors for machinery and gearbox matching

Product Introduction

SMK Transmission supplies MS series aluminum housing three-phase asynchronous motors, single-phase motors and brake motor options for general industrial drive systems. Motors can be supplied separately or matched with SMK worm and hypoid gearboxes.

Advantages

  • Compact aluminum housing with effective heat dissipation
  • IEC-compatible dimensions for common mounting arrangements
  • Low-noise, stable operation for industrial duty
  • Voltage, frequency, pole and brake options subject to model
  • Motor and reducer matching from one supplier

Typical Applications

Conveyors, pumps, fans, packaging machines, agricultural equipment, woodworking machinery, automation systems and gear motor assemblies.

Selection Information

Provide power, voltage, frequency, number of poles or target speed, duty, mounting type, enclosure and ambient conditions. For gearbox matching, also provide output speed and torque.

Frequently Asked Questions

Three-phase or single-phase motor?

Three-phase motors are generally preferred for industrial sites with three-phase power. Single-phase motors suit installations where only single-phase supply is available.

Can SMK match a motor to a gearbox?

Yes. Provide input supply, output speed, torque, duty and mounting needs so the motor and reducer can be checked as a system.

When is a brake motor required?

A brake motor is commonly specified when rapid stopping, position holding or load control is needed. Safety requirements must be assessed for the machine.
