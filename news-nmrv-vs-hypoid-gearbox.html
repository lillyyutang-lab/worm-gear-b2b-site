NMRV vs Hypoid Gearbox: What's The Difference→

Comparison & Selection Guide

July 31, 2026

When selecting a gearbox for industrial machinery, two popular solutions often come into consideration: NMRV worm gearboxes and hypoid gearboxes. Both are widely used for speed reduction and torque transmission, but they have significant differences in efficiency, structure, performance, and application.

1. Gear Structure Difference

NMRV Worm Gearbox: Uses a worm and worm wheel transmission structure. The sliding contact design provides a compact structure and smooth, quiet operation.

Hypoid Gearbox: Uses a hypoid spiral bevel gear structure. Power is transmitted through rolling contact, offering higher efficiency and torque capacity.

2. Efficiency Comparison

Gearbox TypeTypical Efficiency
NMRV Worm Gearbox60%-90%
Hypoid Gearbox90%-95%

Hypoid gearboxes maintain higher efficiency because their gear teeth have less sliding friction, making them ideal for energy-saving applications.

3. Torque Capacity Comparison

For the same frame size, hypoid gearboxes usually provide higher torque density and better load capacity compared with NMRV worm gearboxes, which are better suited for medium torque requirements.

4. Self-Locking Capability

Worm gearboxes (NMRV) can have a self-locking function at high reduction ratios, preventing reverse rotation. Hypoid gearboxes generally do not have this characteristic but offer superior mechanical performance.

Conclusion

NMRV gearboxes remain a top choice for general applications due to their cost-effectiveness. However, if your project requires high efficiency and long-term reliability, a Hypoid gearbox is an excellent investment.
