Worm Gear Motor Manufacturer & Gearbox Motor Assemblies

Matched electric motor and worm reducer assemblies for compact right-angle industrial drives

Quick answer: SMK worm gear motors are complete right-angle drive assemblies combining an NMRV-type worm gearbox with a matched AC, DC, single-phase or three-phase motor. They are selected by the machine's required output speed, continuous and peak torque, duty cycle, mounting arrangement, voltage and environment—not by motor power alone.

Worm Gear Motor Product Introduction

A worm gear motor is used when a machine needs a compact 90-degree drive with a lower output speed and higher usable torque than the motor provides directly. SMK supplies the gearbox and motor as a matched assembly, helping equipment builders reduce interface work between the motor frame, input flange, shaft and reducer.

Typical configurations use an NMRV/RV-style worm reducer with a hollow-bore, solid-shaft or flange output, depending on the selected gearbox size. Motor options may include three-phase, single-phase, brake or DC configurations subject to the model and application. Final ratio, torque capacity, thermal suitability and dimensions must be confirmed against the actual duty point.

These assemblies are commonly specified for conveyors, packaging and filling equipment, food-processing machinery, mixers, feeders, doors, gates, agricultural equipment and other systems where space, reliable speed reduction and straightforward maintenance are important.

Why Buyers Specify a Matched Worm Gear Motor

  • One matched drive package: motor input, reducer interface and output arrangement are checked together.
  • Compact right-angle layout: useful where the motor and driven shaft must turn through 90 degrees.
  • Flexible electrical options: AC, DC, single-phase, three-phase and brake motor configurations are available for applicable models.
  • Application-based selection: ratio, output torque, service factor, starts, reversing and thermal conditions are considered together.
  • OEM purchasing support: SMK can review nameplates, drawings, mounting details, packaging and quantity before quotation.

Typical Applications

Worm gear motors are used in conveyors, packaging equipment, food machinery, doors and gates, agricultural equipment, mixers, feeders and material-handling systems.

How to Specify a Worm Gear Motor

Start with the machine output: target rpm, continuous torque and peak or starting torque. Then provide supply voltage and frequency, motor input speed, operating hours, starts per hour, reversing or shock load, mounting position, shaft or bore dimensions, environmental conditions and quantity. For replacement orders, include the existing motor or gearbox nameplate and clear photos of the installation.

SMK can then check the nearest available ratio, motor configuration, service factor, thermal suitability, mounting and interface. The final drawing and order specification remain the reference for installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a worm gear motor?

It combines an electric motor and worm gearbox to provide a lower output speed, increased torque and a compact right-angle output.

How do I choose the ratio?

Divide motor input speed by required output speed, then verify the nearest available ratio, rated output torque, service factor and thermal capacity.

Can I order the gearbox and motor separately?

Yes. SMK can supply separate components or a matched worm gearbox motor assembly.

What information should I send for a quotation?

Send output speed, continuous and peak torque, motor power, voltage, frequency, duty, mounting position, shaft or bore dimensions, application and quantity.

Can SMK match a worm gear motor to an existing machine?

Yes. Send the existing nameplate, shaft or flange dimensions, installation photos, target output speed and load data so the complete drive can be checked.
