Worm Gear Motor Manufacturer & Gearbox Motor Assemblies

Matched electric motor and worm reducer assemblies for compact right-angle industrial drives

Quick answer: A worm gear motor combines an electric motor and worm gearbox to reduce output speed, multiply usable torque and turn the drive through 90 degrees. Select it from required output rpm, continuous and peak torque, duty, available voltage and frequency, mounting position and environment—not from motor power alone.

Worm Gear Motor Product Introduction

SMK worm gear motors combine an NMRV-type worm gear reducer with a matched AC, DC, single-phase or three-phase electric motor. A complete worm gearbox motor package converts high motor speed into the lower output speed and higher torque required by conveyors, packaging machinery and automation equipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a worm gear motor?

It combines an electric motor and worm gearbox to provide a lower output speed, increased torque and a compact right-angle output.

How do I choose the ratio?

Divide motor input speed by required output speed, then verify the nearest available ratio, rated output torque, service factor and thermal capacity.

Can I order the gearbox and motor separately?

Yes. SMK can supply separate components or a matched worm gearbox motor assembly.

What information should I send for a quotation?

Send output speed, continuous and peak torque, motor power, voltage, frequency, duty, mounting position, shaft or bore dimensions, application and quantity.

Can SMK match a worm gear motor to an existing machine?

Yes. Send the existing nameplate, shaft or flange dimensions, installation photos, target output speed and load data so the complete drive can be checked.
