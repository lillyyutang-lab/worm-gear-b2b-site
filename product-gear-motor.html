Components

1. Housing: Die-cast aluminum alloy gearbox for RV025–RV090; cast iron gearbox for RV110–RV150.

2. Worm wheel: Wearable tin (stannum) bronze alloy or aluminum bronze alloy, subject to model.

3. Worm shaft: 20Cr steel with carburizing, quenching and grinding; surface hardness 56–62 HRC, with approximately 0.3–0.5 mm remaining carburized layer after precision grinding.

4. Input configurations: Compatible configurations may include electric motor, brake motor, DC motor or servo motor, subject to model confirmation.

5. Applicable motors: IEC-normalized AC motors and applicable DC or servo motor configurations.

6. Output configurations: Keyed hollow shaft, hollow shaft with output flange, or plug-in solid shaft output.

7. Options: Worm shaft rear extension, single output shaft, double output shaft, output flange, torque arm and dust cover.

Features

  • Lightweight aluminum alloy gearbox options with corrosion-resistant surface treatment.
  • Two applicable worm-wheel material options: tin bronze or aluminum bronze alloy.
  • Standardized parts with flexible shaft configurations and motor-flange interfaces.
  • Multiple mounting options, low noise and efficient heat dissipation when correctly selected.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a worm gear motor?

A motor and worm reducer combined to provide lower speed, higher torque and a 90-degree output.

How do I choose the ratio?

Divide motor speed by target output speed, then verify torque, service factor and thermal capacity.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Send output speed, torque, motor power, voltage, mounting, duty, application and quantity.

Can I order the motor and gearbox separately?

Yes. SMK can supply separate components or a matched assembly.

Can SMK match an existing machine?

Yes. Send the nameplate, installation photos and shaft or flange dimensions.
