RV30 DIMENSION GUIDE

NMRV030 Dimensions & RV30 Selection Guide

14 mm output bore, ratios 5–100 and motor flange dimensions

Published September 23, 2026 · Reviewed by SMK Transmission

Quick answer: NMRV030 (RV30) is a compact right-angle worm gearbox with a 14 mm hollow output bore. The illustrated configuration uses ratios from 5:1 to 100:1, 9 mm or 11 mm round-flange input bores, and square-flange options for compatible stepper and servo motors. Confirm the final motor flange and installation dimensions before ordering.

How to Select the Right RV30

Confirm required output speed, motor power, motor shaft size, output bore and mounting position. For replacement projects, send the existing gearbox nameplate and installation dimensions. See the NMRV size guide, mounting-position guide and NMRV worm gearbox range for related references.
