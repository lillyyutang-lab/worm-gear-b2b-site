How To Select The Right Worm Gearbox For Conveyor System?

Product Knowledge & Guide

July 31, 2026

Conveyor systems are widely used in industries such as food processing, packaging, agriculture, logistics, and automated manufacturing. As a key component of the conveyor drive system, the worm gearbox directly affects the equipment’s efficiency, reliability, and service life.

Choosing the right worm gearbox requires careful consideration of torque requirements, speed ratio, motor power, installation space, and working conditions. Selecting an unsuitable gearbox may lead to insufficient output torque, overheating, excessive noise, or premature failure.

In this article, we will explain the key factors to consider when selecting a worm gearbox for conveyor applications.

1. Determine The Required Output Torque

Torque is one of the most important parameters when selecting a conveyor gearbox. The gearbox must provide enough torque to overcome:

  • Conveyor load
  • Friction resistance
  • Starting force
  • Material weight
  • Inclined conveyor requirements

The basic formula: Torque (Nm) = Force (N) × Radius (m)

For heavy-duty conveyors, choosing a gearbox with insufficient torque capacity may cause motor overload, reduced gearbox lifetime, or conveyor stopping during operation. Therefore, always select a gearbox with a suitable safety factor according to the application.

2. Calculate The Required Output Speed

The conveyor speed determines the gearbox reduction ratio. Most conveyor systems require relatively low output speeds, usually between 10 RPM and 60 RPM.

Example:
A standard motor speed: 1400 RPM
Required conveyor speed: 35 RPM
The approximate reduction ratio: 1400 ÷ 35 = 40:1

Common worm gearbox ratios include 5:1, 10:1, 20:1, 30:1, 40:1, 50:1, 60:1, 80:1, and 100:1.

3. Select The Correct Motor Power

Motor power depends on conveyor load, operating speed, and working conditions.

ApplicationTypical Motor Power
Small conveyor0.12kW - 0.75kW
Medium conveyor0.75kW - 3kW
Heavy conveyor3kW and above

4. Consider Installation Space And Mounting Position

Before selecting a gearbox, confirm the mounting position, input flange size, and available installation space. For compact conveyor systems, NMRV worm gear reducers with hollow shafts are widely used because of their easy installation and space-saving design.

5. Choose The Right Gearbox Material

  • Aluminum Housing Gearbox: Lightweight, corrosion resistant, suitable for packaging and food machinery.
  • Cast Iron Gearbox: Higher strength, better for heavy loads, suitable for mining and heavy industrial machinery.

Conclusion
