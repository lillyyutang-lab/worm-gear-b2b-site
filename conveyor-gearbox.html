Conveyor Gearbox: Quick Selection Answer

A conveyor gearbox reduces motor speed and provides the torque needed to move the loaded belt, chain or roller system. Start with the required conveyor speed and load, calculate output torque, then verify the gearbox ratio, motor power, service factor, mounting and thermal capacity.

For a faster recommendation, send:
  • Conveyor speed or required output rpm
  • Conveyor load and pulley/sprocket diameter
  • Motor voltage, frequency and available power
  • Operating hours, starts per hour and load type
  • Mounting position and output-shaft requirement
  • Environment: temperature, dust, moisture or washdown

How to Select a Conveyor Gear Motor

1. Define Conveyor Speed

Use belt speed and drive-pulley diameter, or chain speed and sprocket data, to determine required gearbox output rpm. The ratio is then based on motor input speed divided by target output speed.

2. Calculate Required Torque

Consider conveyed load, friction, incline, acceleration and drive radius. Use the calculated torque as a starting point and include a suitable service factor for the actual duty.

3. Verify the Drive Package

Confirm the selected gearbox can deliver rated torque at the chosen ratio and input speed. Then check motor power, mounting, output shaft, thermal limits and environment.

Conveyor Gearbox FAQ

How do I select a conveyor gearbox?

Start with conveyor speed, load, drive diameter, output torque, duty, mounting and environment. Then select the ratio and motor power, and verify rated torque and service factor.

Can an NMRV worm gearbox be used on a conveyor?

Yes. NMRV worm gearboxes are commonly used for compact right-angle conveyor drives when the selected model is within the required torque, service factor and thermal ratings.

What should I send for a conveyor gearmotor quotation?

Send speed, load, pulley or sprocket dimensions, duty cycle, motor supply, mounting, output shaft or flange details and quantity.
