Components

1. Housing:
Aluminium alloy housing for KM050–KM090.
Gray cast iron housing for KM110.

2. Gears:
Helical and hypoid bevel gear set manufactured from 20CrMnTi steel, with carburizing and nitriding treatment and approximately 0.3–0.6 mm remaining carburized layer after precision grinding.
Gear hardness: 58–62 HRC.

3. Input Configurations:
Compatible with electric motors including AC motor, brake motor, DC motor and servo motor.
Hollow shaft input with IEC flange (B5/B14).
Hollow shaft input with NEMA flange.
Hollow shaft input with servo motor flange.
Solid shaft input.

4. Output Configurations:
Keyed hollow shaft output.
Hollow shaft with output flange.
Plug-in solid shaft output.

5. Spare Parts:
Output shaft, output flange, torque arm and dust cover.

6. Gearbox Painting:
Aluminium alloy gearbox with shot blasting, anticorrosion treatment and phosphating, painted in RAL 5010 blue or RAL 7035 silver gray.

Models

KM040–KM110

Features

  • Compact right-angle hypoid transmission for industrial machinery.
  • Rated efficiency above 90% under applicable operating conditions.
  • Approximately 10%–40% efficiency improvement compared with conventional RV series worm gearboxes, depending on ratio and operating point.
  • Modular design with multiple motor, flange and shaft combinations.
  • Mounting dimensions are compatible with corresponding RV series sizes except KM050 and RV050; confirm the certified drawing before replacement.
  • High-efficiency KM series units can replace RV series reducers in a wide range of suitable applications after dimensional and duty confirmation.
