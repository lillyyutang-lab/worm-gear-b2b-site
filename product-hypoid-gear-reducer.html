BKM Hypoid Gear Reducers

High-efficiency right-angle transmission for demanding duty cycles

Quick answer: Choose a hypoid gear reducer when a machine needs an efficient, compact right-angle drive for frequent or continuous operation. Compared with a conventional worm reducer at a similar duty point, a correctly selected hypoid unit can reduce power losses and operating heat. Final selection must still be based on ratio, continuous and peak torque, service factor, mounting position and motor interface.

Product Introduction

The SMK BKM series uses hypoid gearing to deliver right-angle speed reduction with high transmission efficiency. It is an alternative to conventional worm gearboxes when energy consumption, thermal performance or continuous operation is a priority.

Advantages

  • High efficiency and lower operating heat than many worm designs
  • Compact right-angle layout with strong torque capacity
  • Quiet operation and durable gear engagement
  • Solid-shaft and IEC motor input configurations
  • Suitable for frequent or continuous-duty service

Typical Applications

Conveyors, packaging equipment, logistics systems, mixers, food machinery, automation lines and other machinery requiring efficient right-angle power transmission.

How to Select

Confirm input power and speed, target output speed, peak and continuous torque, service factor, duty cycle, mounting orientation and environment. SMK can compare BKM and NMRV options for the application.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I choose a hypoid gearbox?

Choose hypoid gearing when a right-angle layout, high efficiency and continuous-duty performance are important.

Can a BKM gearbox connect to an IEC motor?

Yes. BKM configurations include motor-ready IEC input and other input arrangements; confirm the required flange and shaft dimensions.

Is a hypoid gearbox self-locking?

Hypoid gearboxes are normally backdrivable. Use a brake where load holding is required.
